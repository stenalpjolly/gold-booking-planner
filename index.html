Booking rules

Making charge uses today's rate
Gold bills at the lower of booked or today's rate. Making charge always applies to today's board rate. GST is 3% on gold plus making charge.
Unused advance & 356-day limit
Unused advance is non-refundable and rolls forward. Any balance left after 356 days converts to a gold coin at 15% making charge. Confirm full advance adjustment before billing.
Plain jewellery only & ₹1.90L cash cap
Redeem against plain BIS-hallmarked (HUID) 916 jewellery only (no coins, stones, or special-price items). Cash is capped at ₹1.90L; settle balance via RTGS, IMPS, or UPI.
Resale & tax note
Short-term gains on gold sold within 24 months are taxed at slab rate. Keep showroom GST invoices and hallmark slips together.
