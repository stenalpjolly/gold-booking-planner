Booking rules

MC billed on purchase-day rate (Clause 1)
Gold base uses min(booked, today), while making charge % always applies to today's board rate. GST (3%) applies on Gold + MC.
Partial advance & no-refund clause (Clauses 2 & 4)
Unused advance carries forward without cash refund. Unclosed balance after 356 days auto-books a gold coin at 15% VA. Confirm full advance closure before billing.
No coins & ₹1.90L cash limit (Clause 3)
Redeem against plain 916 HUID jewellery only (no coins or special-price items). Cash is capped at ₹1.90L; pay balance via RTGS/IMPS/UPI.
Resale & tax note
Short-term gains on gold sold within 24 months are taxed at slab rate. Keep showroom GST invoices and HUID slips together.
